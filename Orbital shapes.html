About the Simulation

This visualization calculates exact Hydrogenic Wavefunctions (\(Z=1\)) using stable recurrence relations for generalized Laguerre polynomials and real spherical harmonics.

Coordinate System: This simulation strictly follows the mathematical physics convention where the Z-axis is vertical, and the X/Y axes form the horizontal plane.

Nodal Surfaces (where \(\psi = 0\)) are extracted precisely from analytical roots:

  • Spheres = Radial Nodes
  • Planes / Cones = Angular Nodes

The cloud is sampled via a bounded Monte Carlo method. Density colors map to \(|\psi|^2\) using a non-linear scale (\(\gamma \approx 0.40\)) to enhance visibility of faint outer lobes without distorting physical coordinates.
